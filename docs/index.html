NBA Prop Board

Every leg is at the real DraftKings line. At the real line no prop is a sure thing: books set it near 50/50. In last season's backtest the "Most likely" legs hit about 67% and the "Fair price" legs about 57%, and neither beat DraftKings on profit by more than noise. So treat these as the best-supported picks with honest odds, not a money machine. The track record at the bottom shows whether the numbers hold up.

How a pick is made
  1. Every finished box score is stored. Each player's production per minute is weighted toward recent games; last season counts at half weight.
  2. Minutes are projected from the last ~5 games (blended with last season early on), and trimmed on back-to-backs.
  3. The opponent's tendency to allow each stat adjusts the projection, shrunk toward average until it has enough games.
  4. A player-specific distribution turns the projection into a probability at tonight's DraftKings line. That is blended with DraftKings' own price, using weights learned from last season's real lines and results (the market carries most of the weight; it predicted results better than the model alone).
  5. Two cards. "Most likely": blended chance 58%+, cleared that exact line in 7+ of his last 10, matchup not against him, any price. "Fair price": priced -150 or better, blended chance 52%+, 6+ of last 10, matchup +1% or better. Each card takes every qualifying leg, best first, up to 6. If fewer than 3 qualify it is filled to 3 with the next-best legs, marked "below bar".
  6. Matchup checks. Every stat has its own: rebounds look at the opponent's rebounding share, rebound chances, size and position defense; free throws at the FT rate they allow, their fouling, position defense and home/away; points at shooting allowed, rim protection and the Vegas team total; and so on. Each check is weighted by how much it actually predicted results in past games. Teammates and opponents who are out are part of it: their share of shots, assists, rebounds and minutes is redistributed.
  7. Players who are injured, listed questionable, low-minute or volatile are excluded. The card takes one leg per player and at most two per game.
